Parent account login

Secure sign-in is not yet available. We are preparing email/password authentication and email-based password recovery. Until the server-side identity service is configured and tested, we will not collect passwords or claim accounts are protected.

The parent dashboard is currently a public development sandbox, not a private account. Do not store sensitive student or family information in the preview.

Worksheets and weekly packet previews remain available without an account.
